Hello 

I study how strategic reasoning and cognitive abilities develop across the life cycle.

I hold a PhD in Data Science with International Doctorate Mention from Universidad Loyola Andalucía (Sobresaliente, 2026), with a thesis on the evolution of strategic thinking in adolescence and its relation with cognitive abilities. I use lab-in-the-field experiments to understand how strategic coordination, decision-making and cognitive abilities evolve, especially in children and teenagers. My interest in improving welfare has led me to work as a poverty & inequality specialist at Save the Children and as a public policy analyst evaluating the Minimum Income Scheme. I have also worked extensively as a field coordinator, and I’m open to freelance opportunities coordinating, running and organizing field research and experiments. You’ll find my publications and CV below, or you can get in touch.
